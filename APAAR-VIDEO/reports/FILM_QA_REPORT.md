## Voice check (speech-to-text on every narration segment)
All 12 segments are intelligible and match the script. The only differences are spelling and numerals, not wrong words:
"lakhs" heard as "lacks", "APAAR" (spoken "Ah-paar") written "awpar" / "a par", "sixty-eight" / "sixty" written as digits, "S-I-H two six two six three" written "sih 26263".
Each segment is shorter than its window (6.0–18.5 s of 10–28 s), so no line overruns its scene.

## Honesty checks
- Every screen is a DOM snapshot of the real application, with synthetic seed data. No invented features or statistics.
- Figures shown come from the app: for example 5 institutions, 12 programmes, approved intake 798, within intake 688, Narmada MBA 68 against 60.
- Simulated items are labelled on screen: the APAAR registry check ("Simulated" badge, dashed amber box in the architecture, end-card line), and the public status page.
- No official seals or emblems. The painted ring pulse at signing is not an emblem.
- The word "fraud" is not used. The production website and its data were not modified.

## Known limitations (not hidden)
- Higgsfield had 0 credits and its connector cannot generate; Katana was not available; Gemini image/video quota was 0. Nothing in the film was generated by those tools.
- The mascot, painted scenes and music are code-drawn and synthesized, not hand-painted on paper or recorded.
- The "hand-painted" look is a brush-stroke simulation (wet-edge wash, grain, ink line boiling on twos), not scanned paint.
- Fast-forward over six repetitive row fixes is labelled on screen ("8× · six more fixes").
- The mix was checked by measurement and by a speech-to-text pass; it has not been judged by a person listening.
- Frame-by-frame review was done on contact sheets every 8 s plus targeted stills, not on every frame.
